Over 60 Years of Culinary Tradition

The Hitching Post Restaurant has a 60-year tradition of serving the "World's Best Barbecue" to folks from all over the world. We serve only the finest beef from the Midwest, our specialty since 1952, and also feature fresh seafood, ribs, and chicken. Now you can have our famous food at your special event.

Whether it's traditional Santa Maria Style barbecue for 50 or a plated Filet Mignon dinner for 600, the Hitching Post catering crew will impress you and your guests!

Costs involved for onsite or offsite events vary depending on a number of factors, such as location, number of guests, menu, type of service, etc. We would be happy to give you a quote. You may e-mail us, or call us at (805) 937-6151.

The Hitching Post Full Service Difference

Our 60-year reputation as the premier steakhouse on the Central Coast provides our clients with the peace of mind knowing the last thing they'll have to worry about on the day of their big event is the food!

Your Menu

Choose one of our most popular menus or we will meet with you as often as needed to customize a menu that works for you. Private food tastings can be gladly arranged. Our event coordinator is onsite the day of the event to ensure perfect timing.

Recommendations

Having catered on the Central Coast for over 30 years, we are familiar with many venues and vendors. We can successfully recommend rental companies, florists, bakeries, music, and photographers we've worked with.

Rental Items

We coordinate the renting of china, flatware, glassware, tables, chairs, linen, dance floors, and tents. On the day of the event, we check items in and make sure everything ordered has safely arrived.

Bar Service

We hold a full bar catering license and can provide a fully staffed bar for your guests throughout the duration of your event.

Day of Event Execution

Our staff assists other vendors as needed. Our coordinator stays connected with everyone onsite so everything runs smoothly. Cake cutting by our staff is entirely complimentary.

Clean Up

For an additional fee, our staff remains onsite until the conclusion of your event to take down tables and chairs, remove linens, and ensure glassware is securely stored.

Whatever your special event—wedding, anniversary, birthday, baptism—we want to serve your family and friends.

Whether here at our restaurant or at your location, count on our 60 years of experience to impress your guests!
